      To most travelers, they're just two ordinary words. To cabin crew, they mark
      the beginning of the same 20 seconds they've witnessed thousands of
      times before.


      A passenger steps forward, reaches into their bag... then suddenly freezes.
      They start digging. Wallet. Charger. Sunglasses. Lip balm. Receipts.
      Everything comes out—except the passport they're desperately looking for.
      The boarding line slows down, people begin waiting, and panic quietly takes
      over.


      I never imagined I'd become one of those travelers.


      Standing just a few steps from the aircraft door, I confidently reached into
      my tote for my passport and boarding pass. I knew they were inside. The
      problem was... so was everything else.

      That's when I noticed something I'd somehow overlooked on every flight I'd

      Cabin crew never seemed to search through their own bags. Not while
      boarding. Not during security. Not while walking through the airport.
      Everything they needed appeared instantly, almost like they always knew
      exactly where it was.


      At first, I assumed it was simply because they fly every day. But after
      researching how experienced cabin crew organize their travel essentials, I
      realized it wasn't experience that made the difference.


      It was one surprisingly simple travel solution—a recommendation many
      cabin crew quietly share because it helps eliminate the most stressful 20
      seconds of every journey.
