The perfect bag for every part of your day.
Siena Pro Tote Bag Grande is sleek enough to take to
work
and stylish enough for
after-work activities
. And yes, it’s perfect for those
weekend getaways
too. With smart compartments designed to keep you organized, it adapts to every part of your day.
Smart-storage system.
Impressive capacity.
Perfect organization.
From a padded laptop sleeve and RFID protection to a retractable key leash—this model features a
smart storage system
designed to maintain order and effectively utilize the bag’s entire space. It’s a spacious, versatile companion built for work, travel, and active weekends.
Total volume
22 L
EVA laptop sleeve for maximum protection.
The upgraded Siena Pro Tote Bag Grande boasts a cutting-edge
EVA laptop sleeve
, elevating how you transport your tech. With this new feature, your 15.6” laptop is accessible yet cradled in a shock-absorbent, snug sleeve that guards against bumps and bruises.

Everything in its place.
Main zippered compartment.
Secured with a reliable zip closure, it guarantees the safety of your valuable belongings. Thanks to its impressive capacity, you can easily fit everything necessary for the day, while maintaining the bag’s elegant exterior.
Quick-access pockets.
Located on the right and left, these are perfect for your phone, tickets, and other must-haves—crafted for those moments when time is of the essence.
Hidden anti-theft pocket
that is only accessible to you. It reliably conceals your passport and wallet, allowing you to maintain full control over your most important items.
RFID-blocking card slots.
In an era of electronic fraud, protect your essentials. Integrated RFID-blocking slots ensure your personal information is securely shielded from scanners.
Retractable key reel.
No more lost time or lost keys. The Siena Pro Tote Bag Grande features a key reel ensuring your keys are always accessible and exactly where you left them.
Two interior stretch pockets
are ideally suited for the vertical placement of a bottle or umbrella. Your essentials are always secured and easily accessible, maintaining perfect order within.
Fleece sunglasses pocket.
This pocket is our promise of flawless storage. The soft fleece lining prevents scratching on the lenses. Enjoy every day, knowing your stylish accessory is waiting for you in perfect condition.
TPU zippered pocket.
Made from durable thermoplastic polyurethane, this pocket has a waterproof, easy-to-clean, and transparent surface. It allows you to keep important small items secure, isolating the contents, and always in view.
Pen and lipstick loops
make this tote perfect for the woman on the move. They keep your essentials stylishly at hand, from deal-making to socializing.
Integrated accessory hook.
It provides the perfect, effortless attachment for earbuds or accessories that doesn’t disrupt the overall look. Clean aesthetics, flawless functionality.
Integrated USB charging port.
Connect your power bank to the internal cable and plug your phone into the external Type-C compatible port to charge on the go without opening your bag.
Design.
The two-look tote:
adapts to your world.
Fold-in
Fold-out
Thanks to the unique construction and
side snaps
, the bag easily transforms its silhouette, adjusting to the demands of your day. In “Fold-in” mode, you use the snaps to create a sophisticated trapezoid silhouette. This shape is ideal
for business meetings and a professional, elegant setting
. Unfasten the snaps to achieve a classic, more spacious rectangular shape in “Fold-out” mode. This provides a stylish look
for travel or everyday use
. This is versatility that is always under your control.
