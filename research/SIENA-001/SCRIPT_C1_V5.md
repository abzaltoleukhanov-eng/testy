SCAN IT AND I'LL TELL YOU

Airline check-in counter, international flight. One scene in real time; the camera stays at the counter throughout, apart from one short montage under Carol's voice. A personal-item sizer stands next to the counter. Overhead board: "CHECK-IN CLOSES IN 7 MIN." Under the counter sits Denise's own bag, stuffed, a pair of uniform shoes sticking out.

0:00
A woman, the PASSENGER, stands at the sizer. Her backpack is jammed halfway in: a water bottle bulges out of the side pocket, the front pocket is stuffed round. She's pressing down on it with both hands.
DENISE: Ma'am, you can't push it down. It has to fit on its own. That's gonna be forty-nine dollars, and it gets checked.
PASSENGER: It's half an inch!
DENISE: Then it's half an inch.
The passenger taps her card. Beep.

0:10
Denise sticks a bag tag on the backpack and slides the passenger's passport and boarding pass back across the counter.
DENISE: Any laptops or power banks need to come out. Here you go.
The passenger takes her documents, sits down on the floor by the counter and moves her laptop, charger and a wrapped gift box into a plastic shopping bag.

0:15
DENISE: Next.
CAROL, about 60, steps up with a stuffed tote on her shoulder. She slides her passport out of a hidden back pocket without opening the bag and sets it on the counter. Denise looks at the tote.
DENISE: Can you pop your personal item in the sizer for me?

0:21
Carol lowers the tote into the sizer. Its sides stay flat; nothing bulges. It stops with the top just above the rim. Carol's palm is still resting on top as she lets go, and the bag sinks level with the rim.

0:25
DENISE: You pushed it. That doesn't count. Forty-nine.
CAROL: I was just letting go.
DENISE: Your hand was on top. I saw it. Card?

0:33
CAROL: Can I try that again? No hands this time.
Denise nods toward the passenger on the floor.
DENISE: She didn't get a second try. That's my call.

0:39
Carol looks at the plastic bag on the floor.
CAROL: Last year I paid for half an inch. Right here. Carried my granddaughter's presents onto the plane in a bag like that one. So this time I packed the night before. Made sure it all fit. I even—

0:47
From "So this time" on, Carol's voice continues over a montage of the night before: her kitchen table, warm lamp light, her hands. Fast cuts on the beat:
- a laptop slides into its sleeve;
- a pill organizer goes into the side pocket;
- seven rolled outfits, one after another, chk-chk-chk;
- a wrapped gift laid on top; her palm rests on it for a second;
- the top zip starts to run shut.
The zip is halfway along when:
DENISE (O.S.): Ma'am.

0:54
The montage cuts off on the half-closed zip. Back at the counter, on Denise's face.
DENISE: Card, please.
CAROL: I'm not paying for that. Is there someone else who could take a look?

1:00
DENISE: If I call her over, the line stops. Everyone's gonna be waiting on you.
Carol nods quietly and waits.
The line murmurs. Denise turns toward the far end of the counters.
DENISE: Linda? Got a sec?

1:08
LINDA, the shift supervisor, walks over from the far end.
DENISE: She pushed it in. She won't pay.
Linda looks at the stuffed tote in the sizer, then at Carol.
LINDA: Ma'am, my agent says you pushed it in. That's forty-nine. And heads up, it'll be the same on your way back. Check-in closes in five minutes.

1:21
CAROL: I didn't push it. See for yourself. One drop, no hands.

1:25
Linda glances at the board, then at the line, which has been standing still for a minute.
LINDA: Okay. One drop. But if it doesn't fit, I'm not holding up this line twice. You'll pay at the gate. That's ninety-nine.

1:35
The passenger speaks quietly from the floor.
PASSENGER: Just pay the forty-nine. Don't risk it.
Carol looks at her, then at the plastic bag in her lap. A beat.
CAROL: I'll take my chances.
The passenger shakes her head, gets up with her plastic bag and walks off toward security.

1:42
Denise glances at the line. They're all watching her. She folds her arms.

1:44
Carol lifts the tote out of the sizer and holds it over the opening. The line goes quiet. Linda watches.

1:49
Carol lets go and takes a small step back. The tote drops, catches a corner on the rim for a second and hangs there, crooked. Carol doesn't flinch.

1:52
DENISE: It's not in.
LINDA: Wait.

1:55
The tote settles evenly under its own weight and slides all the way in, flat sides against the frame. The top sits below the rim. Carol hasn't moved.

1:59
Silence.
LINDA: Check her in.
Linda heads back to her counter.

2:03
Denise picks up Carol's passport. Her eyes drop to her own bag under the counter, then go back to the tote in the sizer. She asks quietly.
DENISE: What bag is that?
Carol glances up at the board: "CHECK-IN CLOSES IN 2 MIN."
CAROL: Scan it and I'll tell you.
Denise scans the passport. Beep. The printer spits out a boarding pass.
CAROL: Nordace. The Siena Pro Tote.
Carol takes her bag, her boarding pass and her passport and heads for security.

2:14
Denise writes the name on a scrap of paper and tucks it under her keyboard.
DENISE: Next.

2:18
Packshot: "Nordace Siena Pro Tote. Looks small. Holds a week."
Small print: "Personal-item limits vary by airline. Dramatization."
