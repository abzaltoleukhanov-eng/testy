# V2 · Scan It and I'll Tell You

Airline check-in counter, international flight. One scene in real time; the camera stays at the counter throughout, apart from one short montage under Carol's voice. A personal-item sizer stands next to the counter. Overhead board: "CHECK-IN CLOSES IN 7 MIN." Under the counter sits Denise's own bag, stuffed, a pair of uniform shoes sticking out.

**0:00.** A woman, the PASSENGER, stands at the sizer. Her backpack is jammed halfway in, and she's pressing down on it with both hands.
**DENISE:** Hands off, please. It has to drop in on its own. That's forty-nine, and it gets checked.
**PASSENGER:** It's half an inch!
**DENISE:** Then it's half an inch.
The passenger taps her card. Beep.

**0:08.** Denise sticks a bag tag on the backpack.
**DENISE:** Laptop and batteries out, please.
The passenger sits down on the floor by the counter and moves her laptop, charger and a wrapped gift box into a plastic shopping bag. Her passport stays on the counter in front of Denise.

**0:12.**
**DENISE:** Next.
CAROL, about 60, steps up with a stuffed tote on her shoulder. She slides her passport out of a hidden back pocket without opening the bag and sets it on the counter. Denise looks at the tote.
**DENISE:** Personal item in the sizer, please.

**0:18.** Carol lowers the tote into the sizer. It slides down and stops about an inch short of the bottom. Carol's palm is still resting on top as she lets go, and the bag sinks in.

**0:22.**
**DENISE:** That was pressure. It doesn't count. Forty-nine.
**CAROL:** I was letting go.
**DENISE:** Your hand was on top. I saw it. Card?

**0:28.**
**CAROL:** Could I drop it one more time? No hands.
Denise nods toward the passenger on the floor.
**DENISE:** She didn't get a second try. That's my call.

**0:34.** Carol looks at the plastic bag on the floor.
**CAROL:** Last year, at this counter, I paid for half an inch. And I carried my granddaughter's presents onto the plane in a bag. One just like that. So this year I packed the night before. Everything in its place. Took me all evening. I even—

**0:40.** From "So this year" on, Carol's voice continues over a montage of the night before: her kitchen table, warm lamp light, her hands. Fast cuts on the beat:
- a laptop slides into its sleeve;
- a pill organizer goes into the side pocket;
- seven rolled outfits, one after another, chk-chk-chk;
- a wrapped gift laid on top; her palm rests on it for a second;
- the top zip starts to run shut.

The zip is halfway along when:
**DENISE (O.S.):** Ma'am.

**0:46.** The montage cuts off on the half-closed zip. Back at the counter, on Denise's face.
**DENISE:** Card, please.
**CAROL:** I'm not paying for that. Could I speak to your supervisor, please?

**0:51.**
**DENISE:** If I call her, the line stops. Everyone waits on you.
Carol nods quietly and waits.
The line murmurs. Denise picks up her radio.
**DENISE:** Linda, counter six. Passenger refusing to pay.

**0:59.** LINDA, the shift supervisor, walks over from the next counter.
**DENISE:** She pushed it in. She won't pay.
Linda looks at the stuffed tote in the sizer, then at Carol.
**LINDA:** Ma'am, my agent saw pressure. It's forty-nine. And it'll be the same on your return flight. Check-in closes in five minutes.

**1:09.**
**CAROL:** I didn't push it. Watch it yourself. Once, no hands.

**1:13.** Linda glances at the board, then at the line, which has been standing still for a minute.
**LINDA:** Fine. Once. But if it doesn't go in, I'm not taking payment here, the line's backed up as it is. You'll pay at the gate. That's ninety-nine.

**1:21.** The passenger speaks quietly from the floor.
**PASSENGER:** Pay the forty-nine here. Don't risk it.
Carol looks at her. A beat.
**CAROL:** All right.

**1:27.** Denise said "I saw it" in front of the whole line. Backing down now means losing.
**DENISE:** This time, just let it go. Please don't help it in.
Linda doesn't contradict her agent. She looks at Carol.
**CAROL:** Of course.

**1:33.** Carol lifts the tote out of the sizer and holds it over the opening. The line goes quiet. The passenger on the floor stops packing. Denise folds her arms. Linda watches.

**1:39.** Carol lets go and takes a small step back. The tote drops, catches a corner on the rim and hangs there, crooked.

**1:43.**
**DENISE:** It's not in.
**LINDA:** Wait.

**1:46.** The tote slowly settles under its own weight and slides into the sizer. The top sits below the rim.

**1:50.** Silence.
**LINDA:** Check her in.

**1:53.** Denise picks up Carol's passport. Her eyes drop for a second to her own bag under the counter. She asks quietly.
**DENISE:** What bag is that?
**CAROL:** Scan it and I'll tell you.
Denise scans the passport. Beep. The printer spits out a boarding pass.
**CAROL:** Nordace. The Siena Pro Tote.
Denise writes the name on a scrap of paper.

**2:01.** Linda looks at the tote in the sizer.
**LINDA:** What's in it?
**CAROL:** A week.
Linda leans toward Denise and says quietly:
**LINDA:** Make it two.
Carol takes her bag, her boarding pass and her passport and heads for security.

**2:07.** The passenger gets up off the floor with the plastic bag. Denise hands her her passport and boarding pass.
**PASSENGER:** I fly back Sunday. With the same backpack. What's it called?
Without looking up, Denise reads from her scrap of paper.
**DENISE:** N-O-R-D-A-C-E.

**2:14.** Packshot: "Nordace Siena Pro Tote. Holds more than it looks."
Small print: "Personal-item limits vary by airline. Dramatization."
