# Notes B (work tote threads: 1wgeawi, 1wwpn82, 1x4jobr, 1n46b0x, 18xuicb, 1tdkdv2, 1rr9w0h)

- **Weight with a laptop is the most common reason people give for leaving a leather tote.** Several people describe the same sequence: they liked or owned a leather/structured tote, started carrying a laptop, and moved to nylon or a backpack. Examples: RD-1wwpn82 OP (leather bag "just too heavy with lap top", moved to Tumi), RD-pgh8fzm (leather tote heavy before it is even filled, now Le Pliage / Damson Madder), RD-nbjhbth (Cuyana tote for over a decade, switched to an Aer backpack "to save my back", would still "go tote all the way"), RD-kg6utoo (Kate Spade saffiano tote "rarely" used because of shoulder pain), RD-nbmlypg (loves her Elliker tote, may "go back to a backpack"). Empty weight is also a reason to reject a bag before buying: Cuyana Forma at 3.6 lb (RD-1wgeawi), RSVP Parachute worry for a 5ft1 frame (RD-oa9u58b).
- **The tote is still the preferred look; the backpack is the bag people settle for.** Interpretation: the backpack wins on the body, not on identity. Backpack users call it "bulky and not very professional looking" (RD-18xuicb OP) or "more masculine" (RD-kg6utoo). Nicer totes are tied to a professional job (RD-nbj18d1) or to looking "like a regular purse" (RD-kg71xkx). A casual backpack can be accepted when it is "trendy" or "classy enough" (RD-kg7dkc5, RD-kg6oqk3).
- **People use two bags by situation instead of one bag for everything.** One person takes a light Longchamp daily and a Mulberry to meetings (RD-om1l0a5). Others use the backpack for laptop days and a purse or tote on light days (RD-nbiore4, RD-nbiymgl, RD-nbkcp6w). Some carry a separate handbag or a separate laptop bag every day (RD-1wwpn82 OP, RD-kg70xvw), and one carries three bags because moving her things between them is a chore (RD-nbjwyu5). "One bag" designs are valued for this reason: the MinkeeBlue "three bag shuffle" (RD-kg9na45), a purse kept inside a weekender (RD-kg6xz11), a backpack with a lunch compartment (RD-kg7359y).
- **Durability problems, mostly straps and handles, are a common reason to start looking for a new bag.** Examples: BEIS deteriorating or falling apart in about a year (RD-1x4jobr, RD-nbj5c44), handles fraying in a year (RD-18xuicb), thin straps failing first (RD-1tdkdv2 OP), and a Kanken given up over thin straps (RD-nbj3rso). On the positive side, owners mention years of service (Coach Willow, Tumi backpack since 2017, Lo & Sons "five years", LeSportsac "20 years").
- **Distrust of faux leather is mostly about durability, not about how it looks.** RD-pgpdy76 says "Beis does not hold up because it's not real leather" and recommends real leather or nylon with leather trim. The BEIS owner who complains about deterioration still "really loved the structure and material" (RD-pgekq96) and asks for "leather or leather-like" (RD-1x4jobr), which is a contradiction worth noting. Interpretation: for a vegan-leather tote at about $190, a claim about how long it lasts may matter more than a claim about how it looks. The only Mat & Nat (vegan brand) complaint is about handle length with a winter coat (RD-18xuicb OP), not about the material.
- **Bad after-sale service raises the stakes on price.** RD-nbj5c44 paid over EUR 100. When the bag failed, the brand refused help because she had bought it through a third-party website, and the website refused a return as "fair wear and tear". Her verdict: "the style was perfect, but the quality and customer service didn't match the price tag." A warranty is mentioned as a reason to trust a brand (Briggs & Riley covers damage, not just defects, RD-olvwl3o). Interpretation: these are relevant to the RQ5 distrust question, but they come from single individuals.
- **Many people get premium totes secondhand or vintage to pay less.** Examples: Poshmark (RD-p9tnox2, RD-kg7dkc5), TRR after Senreve closed (RD-pdswhup), eBay (RD-1rr9w0h OP), thrifted or vintage (RD-kg7wlgs, RD-pdo2g0h), and secondhand Ralph Lauren (RD-oa0mvng). Cheap marketplace bags also earn praise ("Lovevook ... on Amazon ... compliments every day", RD-perxtgl). Interpretation: these buyers are the competition for a $188 DTC tote, but the threads say nothing about how common this is.
- **Nordace appears twice, both times as a backpack and both times positively.** RD-nbjeciu says "doesn't feel bulky", "4 women at work have bought one because of mine" (word of mouth). RD-kg8ze4v recommends the Siena Smart Backpack for its compartments. There is no evidence about the tote and no distrust of Nordace in these threads.
- **The meaning of "work bag" differs from person to person.** For one person it is a look she actively avoids. RD-1wgeawi OP left work for health and caregiving: she rejected the Kaai because it "reads too business", avoids black because it "was my standard office wear", and her laptop backpack is "not a me" bag. Others see the work look as a requirement: "professional and not too casual, but does not need to be formal" (RD-1wwpn82), "chic, understated" with "some structure" (RD-1rr9w0h). Interpretation: messaging that shouts "office" could put off people who carry a laptop outside a job.
- **Logo fatigue and returns without a stated reason.** RD-1rr9w0h OP sold two Neverfulls because she is "moving away from big logos", and RD-o9yp61q sold a Neverfull for RSVP Paris. The same OP returned a Staud tote with "it just wasn't it" and gave no reason, so the reasons for returns are still unknown.
- **Must-have features that people actually name:** a top zip (RD-pdo2g0h, RD-pgekq96), a crossbody or long strap for heavy loads (RD-1wwpn82, RD-p9u8r1t, RD-kgau4f4: no handles-only bag on the bus), wider straps for comfort (RD-pgeetnx), feet so the bag stands on the floor (RD-pabji00), fitting under a plane seat (RD-kg6xz11, RD-o9z4ln8), and water-bottle pockets (RD-nbixcwc). Remote work ended the use of a work bag in two cases (RD-pdqajq6, RD-kg9na45).
