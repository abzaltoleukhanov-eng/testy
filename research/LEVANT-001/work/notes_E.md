# Notes, batch E (threads 1jfa4c6, 1q6rkkl, 1aqqf8j, 1w0zzdx, 1h6vjo2, 1vwu0km, 1sajnvc, 1psoc8d, 1injwsv)

- **The usual way out of tote shoulder pain is to switch to a backpack, and people describe it as giving something up.** Several people with their own stories gave up a tote they loved: RD-nyau40t (Kate Spade left in its dust bag after a massage therapist's warning), RD-kqgkwly, RD-kqhlmr2 ("didn't get a stylish backpack but it doesn't matter"), RD-kqg0vb3 (pain gone, though a standing desk was added at the same time), RD-nybhrg5, and RD-ny9xed5 ("make peace with"). Interpretation: the emotional cost is losing the look. Nobody said the backpack failed them physically, with one exception (next bullet).
- **One person found backpacks hurt too.** RD-nycvpon has neck and shoulder issues. Backpacks hurt her over time, so she now splits the load between two shoulder bags. RD-1q6rkkl tried crossbody, carrying the laptop by hand, two bags and a backpack, and every option failed for pain or for looks. So "just get a backpack" does not solve it for everyone.
- **Many people split the load instead of replacing the bag:** a second reusable tote (RD-nyaeaq5), backpack plus crossbody (RD-ny9zz53, RD-1aqqf8j), a two-bag method (RD-p6ho35p, RD-p6hlyrh), an organizer insert that "surprisingly" helped with weight (RD-kqf6f7s), a thicker aftermarket strap (RD-p6hngrh), and leaving the laptop at the office (RD-nyb2rl9). Interpretation: there is room for a single bag that gives the relief of splitting the load, but this batch does not show that people would pay for that.
- **The weight of the bag itself versus the weight of what is in it.** Some people check the bag's empty weight: RD-nyaeaq5 says to check it "first things first", RD-p6h6a8s puts BÉIS at "2.75 pounds before you put anything in it", and the listicle RD-1vwu0km says a bag "close to 1 kg can become uncomfortable". Others say the contents are the problem: RD-kqfsfp0 (her Neverfull "isn't too heavy", it's the bottle, tumbler and planner) and RD-p6hgyaf. Lévant's stated 1 kg sits right at the level RD-1vwu0km calls borderline. That post is generic advice that may be content marketing, so treat it as a way the issue gets framed, not as consumer evidence.
- **A structured bag reads as a heavy bag, but structure also has a visual job.** RD-nyjr2g2 dropped a Tumi backpack because it was "so structured" it was "too heavy even when empty". Yet RD-mipghhx rejected Longchamp because it "lacked structure", and RD-mcbqqwi's soft Poppy & Peonies tote "would not stay standing" with a 15-inch laptop and creased. Interpretation: structure looks good but costs weight, which is the trade-off the Lévant's 1 kg sits on.
- **Lightweight nylon wins on use but loses on looks.** RD-p6hlyrh bought leather totes for a new job, then a Le Pliage, and now "rarely" uses anything else because it is light. RD-p6hagua says "Nylon is lighter than leather." But RD-mipe4ry rejects Longchamp because it looks like a "cheap nylon bag", and RD-1jfa4c6 asks "Is my only option non-leather?" Interpretation: there may be an opening for a bag that looks like leather but is lighter. This is a hypothesis; no one here says vegan leather is lighter.
- **People expect vegan leather to peel, and that expectation sets a low price ceiling.** RD-oe5htkj would "never pay full price or over $70 for vegan leather". RD-oeh3b3o would "NOT repurchase" after a trench coat peeled within 2 years. RD-1injwsv excluded Freja as "too pricey for vegan leather" and BÉIS because reviews mentioned peeling. RD-1psoc8d hesitated on a high price for vegan when real leather costs "just a bit more". Caveat: thread 1sajnvc is about jackets, not bags. Even so, the belief carries over: several people say "all vegan leather" peels (RD-nvdw75x, RD-mcbmxek, RD-mcc5pxt). This matters directly for a vegan tote at about $188.
- **There are counterexamples on durability, and a long-term owner's account helped someone buy.** RD-nvj73z8 has a microfiber vegan leather bag with no cracking at 4.5 years (2.5 to 3 years of daily use according to RD-o1k6soh). After reading that, the poster bought the vegan Harlow (RD-nvjgt20; the link between the two is inferred). Also: RD-mcbqqwi (no peeling after 1 year), RD-mcd3zr2 (Matt & Nat), RD-mcia4ah. Interpretation: specific owner accounts of years without peeling may counter the fear. Microfiber or PU type is named as the difference.
- **Distrust shows up as checking up on a brand, not as outright rejection.** RD-nywhefa looked at a used Cuyana on a resale site and saw the unlined leather pilling. She found Quince and the unknown Poppy & Peonies "hit or miss" in reviews. After seeing about 300 bags she bought an "ok" Amazon bag as a stopgap and put off the real purchase. RD-p6hngrh adds "If I was a shill..." before recommending a bag, which suggests the community expects paid promotion. RD-p6h6a8s uses "Chat" (an AI assistant) to shortlist bags by weight. Nordace is not mentioned anywhere in these threads.
- **Keep or return.** RD-1h6vjo2 is unsure about keeping a Cuyana bought on sale and asks strangers, but gives no reason. RD-nyleo4k returned a Dagne Dover Dakota because it was bulkier than expected, so size and bulk caused the return, not weight. RD-pag91vk returned a suede Fbrq; that weight played a part is only inferred. This batch has no clear return caused by bag weight.
- **People don't want to look sporty.** "Home room" and "bookbag" (RD-nywhefa), "masculine" (RD-1w0zzdx), "off for a trek" (RD-nyawvbc). Rolling bags are ruled out for public transport because of stairs (RD-1w0zzdx). The 15-inch laptop versus a small frame (RD-nywhefa) and the 14-inch MacBook Pro at about 3.5 lb (RD-1w0zzdx) are the actual loads people name.
